# Figure contract

Backend: existing Python/matplotlib workflow. Quantitative grid; all numerical sources are CSV/JSON in analysis.

Question: are measured paired differences and their uncertainty compatible with the predicted reduction of feedback bias above the control bandwidth?
This is a hypothesis, not a predeclared positive conclusion.

Main draft: A and C++++ panels, all three low-frequency and four selected high-frequency damping components.
Log frequency axis 9–550 Hz; observed and predicted points each carry period-jackknife 1SE.
Controller color fixed. Filled/hollow markers distinguish observed/predicted.
Signed symlog y axis with an explicitly labelled linear neighborhood preserves both large low-frequency and small high-frequency values.
M inverse and curvature-corrected predictions get separate complete figures. No results selected for better agreement.
Shaded reference bandwidth is derived from three nonzero loop singular-value unity crossings, not a hard bias-free boundary.
No line connects different mechanical modes as if they constituted one response curve.

Supporting figure: high-frequency linear-y close-up to reveal differences hidden by low-frequency scale.
Reproducibility: pooled within-controller repeat SD vs same-record/B-noise conditional standard uncertainty; only three controller pairs.

Export: PNG 300dpi and PDF editable text, white background, restrained blue/teal/purple colors, final checks for clipping and legend ambiguity.
Failures and original low-frequency strict-score limitations remain stated in captions/report.
